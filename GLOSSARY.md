# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the words for the money side of a purchase.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to get back the money for one purchase. A purchase can have only one Refund Request. Only a Cadence admin approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Pending Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money that goes back to the learner after an admin approves a Refund Request. A Refund is always the full Refund Amount. An Approved Refund Request also ends the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, partial refund

**Requested**:
The status of a Refund Request that nobody has approved or declined yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Open, Raised

**Approved**:
The status of a Refund Request that ends in a Refund.

**Declined**:
The status of a Refund Request that ends with no Refund. The learner keeps the course. Declined is final.

**Refund Eligibility**:
A purchase has Refund Eligibility until the end of day 14, and while the learner has watched less than 2 hours of its video. The purchase day is day 0. A claimed team seat never has Refund Eligibility.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, completion limit

**Refund Amount**:
The amount the learner paid for the purchase, after any purchasing-power discount or coupon. It is never the list price.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute that a card holder opens with their bank, outside Cadence. The bank takes the money back. A Chargeback is never a Refund Request.
